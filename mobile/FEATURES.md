# What's new in Maslul

Features added on top of the original app.

## New features

- **The rewrite: Android and iPhone.** Maslul is rebuilt as one app that runs on both Android and iPhone. On Android it installs as an update to the old app and brings over everything the user had saved: Home and Work, favourites, shuttles and settings.
- **Hebrew.** The whole app is available in Hebrew, laid out right to left. It follows the phone's language, or can be chosen in Settings. Stop, place and map names follow the app's language where the data has them.
- **Favourite stops on Home.** Starred stops appear on the Home screen with their next departures, nearest stop first, refreshed every minute while Home is open.
- **Reminder to leave from a stop.** Long-press a departure on a stop's screen (or under a starred stop on Home) to be reminded when to leave, allowing for the walk to the stop.
- **Last bus warning.** In the evening, and on Friday afternoons before Shabbat, the night's last departure for a trip is tagged "Last today", with a banner once it's within the hour.
- **Last routes offline.** The routes from the last five trips are saved. When a search fails, with no connection or the planner down, the saved routes are shown instead, without buses that have already left.
- **Stops away.** While waiting for a live bus, the trip screen and the stop's screen say how many stops away it is, and the "board soon" alert also comes when it's two stops away.

## Improvements

- **Times far ahead carry their day** ("Bus 5 at Sun 21:05" instead of "in 1208 min"), on route cards, route details, shared trips and reminders.
- **Reminders arrive on time:** if Android may delay them, the app offers to turn on "Alarms & reminders".
- **Live directions are steadier:** the bus's position along the route is more accurate, "Get off now" comes at the right stop, the guide moves on to the walk after getting off, and "You've arrived" stays put.
- **Clearer errors:** a failed route search says whether the planner returned an error, took too long, or couldn't be reached.
- **Line search works over weekends**, falling back to the latest timetable data.
- **Shuttles:** days show as ranges ("Sun–Fri"), deleting asks first, and reminders name the shuttle.
